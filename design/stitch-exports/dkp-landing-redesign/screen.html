Role-Based Workspaces

Built for Every Role on Campus

Six access levels, each with its own dashboard, permissions, and tools — enforced on every request.

Members

Borrow books, place holds, and build a personal reading wishlist from the full catalog.

Student Authors

Publish course projects to the university showcase with advisor review and feedback.

Researchers

Submit publications and datasets with DOIs, citations, and lab affiliations.

Archivists

Preserve institutional documents with versioning and tiered access control.

Librarians

Issue and return books, manage fines, and keep the lending catalog moving.

Administrators

Approve accounts, audit every sensitive action, and configure the platform.
